## 1. Summary

- Scope: 453 SKILL.md (435 top-level dirs + nested qdrant ones), 224 agents, 193 instructions, 8 hooks, 8 workflows.
- The 2026-10-02 review (`archive/docs/research/third-party-skills-review.md`) already graded 441 of these skills, and most of its picks were adapted and trimmed into archived plugins (`archive/plugins/{github,github-admin,api,aws,mcp,deploy,infra,security,testing,review,authoring,observability,tooling,git,...}`). Those archive versions are shorter and Claude-shaped, so the right verdict for the awesome-copilot source is **merge → the archive skill (archive wins)**. Re-porting from awesome-copilot would redo work.
- Since that review, 12 skills were added upstream. Two are in scope: `cypress-flake-fixer` (port) and `rebase-current-branch` (merge). The prior review did not look at agents, instructions or hooks. Three of those are worth taking: the `devcontainers` instruction, the `trojan-skill-hunter` agent and the `terratest-module-testing` agent.
- Verdicts by row: **port 3** (new focused plugins), **merge 45 rows** (covering about 95 source files, nearly all into archive ports), **skip** everything else: about 345 skills (mostly vendor or out of stack), about 205 agents, about 180 instructions and all 8 hooks.
- Headline picks:
  - `devcontainer`: a skill built from `instructions/devcontainers.instructions.md` plus `skills/github-codespaces-efficiency`. It is sourced and precise, and nothing in the archive covers it.
  - `plugin-vetter`: an agent built from `agents/trojan-skill-hunter.agent.md` that statically vets third-party skills, agents, hooks and MCP configs. It matches what this repo does.
  - `cypress-flake-fixer`: a skill with a zero-dependency scanner and good references.
- Reviving the archive GitHub cluster (issues, issue-breakdown, pr, pr-comments, release, actions, dependabot, code-security, repo-setup) captures most of awesome-copilot's remaining value. The cluster is big enough to justify a `github-meta`.

## 4. Open questions

1. Archive revival: almost every awesome-copilot pick already exists as a trimmed archive skill. Should the archive reviewer's verdicts on those plugins (github, github-admin, api, aws, mcp, deploy, infra, security, testing) decide them, with this file only confirming that no newer upstream material changes them? Only `rebase-current-branch` and the terratest content are newer.
2. A `github-meta` (issues, issue-breakdown, pr, pr-comments, release, actions, dependabot, code-security, repo-setup): nine focused plugins is enough for a project-scope meta, rather than putting any of them in baseline. Is that wanted?
3. Should `plugin-vetter` be a standalone agent, or should its unicode, encoded-payload and tool-shadowing checks be merged into a revived `agent-config-audit` skill? A separate read-only agent keeps the untrusted content out of the main context, which is why I lean toward the agent.
4. `devcontainer` and `cypress-flake-fixer` fit no current meta. Install them directly, or start a `new:devenv-meta` and a broader `new:frontend-testing-meta`?
