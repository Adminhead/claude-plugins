The user shared a wireframe of a settings screen, described in words, at a very early stage. Judge only the final message.

Identify the points the response treats as most important: its top recommendations, or the points given the most space if it does not rank them.

PASS only if all of these hold:

1. At least two of the three most important points are about how the screen is organised or what it is for: grouping or ordering of the ten rows, where the destructive or rarely used rows sit, the gear icon on a settings screen, how a user finds the setting they came for, or what is missing from the structure.
2. The row spacing the user asked about is answered briefly, or deferred until later in the process. It is not one of the top three points.
3. The response does not give measurements, colour values or typography advice for things the user said are not decided yet.

FAIL otherwise. Do not reward length, and do not penalise a response for being brief about spacing.
