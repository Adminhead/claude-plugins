## What the study was

248 sites from One Page Love (156) and Awwwards Sites of the Day (92), captured on 5 October 2026 in
headless Chrome at 1440x900 and 390x844, measured in the page and scored by 21 model reviewers the
Awwwards way (design 40%, usability 30%, creativity 20%, content 10%). These are model judgements from
still frames, not human jurors. "Top" means a panel score of 7.5 or more (82 measurable sites), "weak"
under 6.5 (23). One capture per site, one day: it records fashion as much as quality.

Monospace, index numbering and thin rules are *more* common on strong sites. Here they are warnings,
not bans: they are what generators copy first, so keep one only when the content calls for it.

## Patterns worth knowing

- **Ideas come from the subject.** The weakest entries' ideas were recorded as "none beyond the standard
  formula"; the strongest were sentences only that subject could own.
- **Hero types (top / weak):** full-bleed media with type over it 16% / 10%; giant wordmark 14% / 0%;
  typographic poster 13% / 7%; centred statement with media below 7% / 24% (the weak sites' signature).
- **Typefaces:** one family on 31% of sites, two on 45%. Strong sites push extremes of scale.
- **Usability does not rise with the rest:** the best sites are no easier to use. Do not copy their
  gates, preloaders, tiny text (9–13px on about 24 of the top 90) or hidden navigation.
- **Pricing, testimonials and FAQ** were picked as a site's most interesting section only 7, 12 and 9
  times in 235: where strong sites have them, they avoid the stock form.
- **Endings:** the name at full width, the hero repeated inverted, or a closing scene; link-column
  footers were mentioned only as criticism.
- **Motion (inferred from frames):** autoplaying video on about 50 of 114 sites at 7.3+, pinned stages
  about 40, text reveals about 30; about 10 strong sites show little or no motion at all.
- **How weak sites fail:** the category's stock template, a centred headline with pill badge, two
  buttons and a screenshot, stock modules after a distinctive hero, a characterless default face, text
  too small or faint, never saying what the thing is.

## How the skill was tested

Blind A/B/C comparisons of builds with and without the procedure, screenshots judged by a separate
agent with the juror brief. In those tests the page that won each brief was built from an item on the
subject inventory, and a richer page with tells beat a clean but thin page every time.
